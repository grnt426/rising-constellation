It is the [[infrastructure-building|infrastructure building]] of a barren planet, and it only goes on that planet's [[infrastructure-tile|tile 1]]. The planet's other tiles take no building until it is finished, and no building there can ever [[upgrades|rise above its level]]. It can never be [[destroy-building|destroyed]], and a [[siege]] never damages it.
